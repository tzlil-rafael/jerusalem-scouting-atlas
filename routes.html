Optimisation, assumptions and unresolved access

Six denser route groups with three or four new targets each, not a guaranteed six-day completion forecast. Eight-hour field-time cap. A fourth target is conditional; defer unfinished work rather than exceed the cap or omit essential field control. No commute to the first target or from the final target is assumed. Between-group target-to-target links are assigned to the following group so no transfer disappears from the sequence; they do not prescribe overnight accommodation or the crew's actual morning origin. Ordering uses a multi-start nearest-neighbour and 2-opt geographic-distance heuristic from target #49. It screens backtracking, not road travel, legal access or a proven global optimum. Preserve modelled road evidence only for matching directed endpoint pairs; all other links stay explicitly schematic. More targets per group do not establish that the group fits eight hours. Geographic sequence length changes from 71.2 to 40.8 km; these are straight-line comparisons, NOT driving distances.
